==============================================================================
A. THE RECOMMENDATION
==============================================================================

  RETRIEVE with markdown_section + BM25 at depth 15, costing
           ~571 estimated context tokens per query.
           Retrieval is OPTIONAL at this corpus size. Sending all 45 articles
           gave the same refusals and answer rate, and correctness
           +0.034 [-0.041, +0.109] (Phase 8). It is kept on cost and headroom.

  PILOT    the `cited` generation layer with a person in the loop.
           It refused 25 of 25 unanswerable questions, invented no
           citations, and scored 0.984 faithfulness on the 76 answers
           it gave. That is enough to put it in front of support agents
           and not enough to put it in front of customers unattended.

  DO NOT   ship the `naive` prompt in any form. It answered 16 of 25
           questions the help centre cannot answer, 14 of them with
           claims the judge found unsupported.

  The line between "pilot" and "ship" is drawn by sample size, not by
  the scores. 25 of 25 supports a true refusal rate of at least 86.7%;
  it cannot exclude about one unanswerable question in 8 being
  answered. For a billing assistant that is not a deployable bound.

C. EVIDENCE — GENERATION  (recorded)
==============================================================================

  Generator gpt-4o-mini, judged by <LLMJudge name='llm_judge_gpt-4o'>.
  The judge scored 100% on 9 validation cases before any of its
  scores were used (the lexical judge it replaced scores 50%).

  arm                    refuse OOS  answer in  faithful  correct  fab. cites
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  extractive_baseline         56.0%      62.1%     0.810    0.268           0
  llm_naive                   36.0%     100.0%     0.811    0.795           0
  llm_cited                  100.0%      80.0%     0.984    0.733           0

  1. The LLM earns its cost over the baseline. Judged correctness
     0.733 against 0.268 for the extractive answerer.

  2. The refusal instruction is what separates the arms. `llm_naive` declined
     9 of 25 unanswerable questions in its own words and answered the
     other 16; 14 of those answers contain claims the judge found
     unsupported. `llm_cited` refused all 25.

  3. When both arms answer, they are equally faithful. The headline
     gap (0.984 vs 0.811) is composition: each arm is averaged over
     the questions IT chose to answer. On the 76 both answered,
     0.984 vs 0.970: -0.015 [-0.040, +0.010] (llm_naive minus llm_cited).

  4. Refusing has a measured price. Correctness, llm_naive minus llm_cited:
       in-scope      +0.160 [+0.102, +0.219]
       out-of-scope  -0.308 [-0.496, -0.116]
     The two effects nearly cancel overall (+0.062 [-0.007, +0.133]),
     which is why a single correctness number would have hidden both.

  5. Over-refusal looks like a retrieval problem. `llm_cited` declined 19 of
     95 answerable questions; the evidence was fully retrieved for
     10, partly for 6 and not at all for 3. So 9 of the 19 were
     declined without the full evidence in hand. Whether missing
     evidence CAUSED them is a separate question, and Phase 8 tests
     it by supplying every article. 10 of the 15 ambiguous questions
     were refused.

==============================================================================
D. HOW MUCH THE EVIDENCE CAN BEAR
==============================================================================

  1. THE SAFETY NUMBER IS A SMALL SAMPLE. 25 of 25 is a lower bound of
     86.7% on the true refusal rate, on questions written by the
     same person who wrote the corpus.

  2. THE JUDGE IS VALIDATED, NOT CERTIFIED. 9 cases with known verdicts
     is a floor test. On the bias probes the longer answer scored
     lower on 2 of 3 probes (-0.222 mean), and the judge's stated reasons
     name specific added claims rather than length. Checked against
     real answers 28 words apart, a per-word penalty predicts
     -0.171 and the data shows -0.015 [-0.040, +0.010].
     Agreement with the lexical judge is kappa -0.465, which counts
     against the lexical judge. No second validated rater exists.

  3. THE JUDGE IS LENIENT ON OMISSION. On the planted contradiction
     (mh-011) `llm_naive` is `states_one` and `llm_cited` is `states_neither`,
     and the judge scored them 0.80 and 0.80 against a reference
     that names the conflict. It checks the claims an answer makes; a
     missing caveat is not a false claim.

  4. ONE MODEL, ONE RUN, ONE CORPUS. Temperature 0, a single generator
     version, 45 synthetic articles. None of the numbers above says
     anything about a different model or a real help centre.

==============================================================================
E. KNOWN FAILURE MODES, RANKED BY DEPLOYMENT RISK
==============================================================================

  Ranked by cost to a customer, not by frequency.

  1. TWO SOURCES DISAGREE AND THE ANSWER DOES NOT SAY SO.
     mh-011: `llm_cited` is `states_neither`, `llm_naive` is `states_one`. No judged
     arm states both refund windows. This is a corpus defect a prompt
     cannot repair, and it is the one failure the judge under-scores.

  2. ANSWERING AN UNANSWERABLE QUESTION. Not observed for `llm_cited`
     (25 of 25 out-of-scope refused), and bounded only at 86.7%.

  3. AN UNSUPPORTED CLAIM IN A CITED ANSWER. 3 of 76 answers fell below
     the faithfulness threshold. A citation makes an answer checkable,
     not correct.

  4. OVER-REFUSAL. 19 of 95 answerable questions declined. A silent
     failure: the customer gets nothing, and no hallucination metric
     records it.

  5. AMBIGUOUS QUESTIONS. Retrieval reaches 22% of its precision
     ceiling there, and generation then refuses most of them. Query
     understanding, which neither retrieval tuning nor prompting fixes.

The deliverable is reports/decision_memo.md. This notebook exists to
keep it honest.

1. THE RECOMMENDATION HAS THREE PARTS, AND THEY REST ON DIFFERENT
   AMOUNTS OF EVIDENCE.
   Retrieval: the configuration is measured across the full sweep
   with paired inference and held-out selection, and Phase 8 found it
   optional at this corpus size. Pilot cited generation: measured,
   on a sample too small to bound the failure that matters. Do not
   ship the naive prompt: it answers most questions it has no evidence
   for.

   A clean sweep on 25 questions is reported with its Wilson lower
   bound, because the count alone reads as a guarantee it is not.

   The contradiction no arm surfaces is one question and ranks first.

4. EVERY NUMBER IS CHECKED, AND THE CHECK SAYS HOW.
   56 claims: 11 recomputed live, 45 against committed records.
   CI fails on drift in either.

The memo's seven phases are complete. Phases 8 and 9 test its
conclusions: 08 against using no retrieval at all, 09 against a stated
cost of error.
